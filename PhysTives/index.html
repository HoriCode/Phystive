Small moves, wherever you are.

Pick an easy option that suits your space, schedule, and comfort. A movement break does not need special equipment.

01 🪑

Desk-side reset

Stand if comfortable, roll your shoulders gently, and reach your arms overhead—or do a small seated stretch.

At your desk or study space

02 🚶

Take a short stroll

Walk along a clear indoor route, take a few steps in place, or move around your classroom or office safely.

Indoors or in a safe nearby area

03 🌱

Move with your community

Invite a classmate, coworker, or neighbor to take a gentle stretch or easy movement break together.

With a buddy or group

A gentle reminder

Keep breaks comfortable and practical.

Choose a clear space and movements that feel comfortable to you. Seated options count, too. In hot or rainy weather, consider a cool, sheltered indoor spot. Take a pause whenever you need one; this page is for general education, not medical advice.

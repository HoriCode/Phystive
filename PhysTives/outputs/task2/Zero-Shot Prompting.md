**Technique:**  
Zero-shot prompting

**Exact Prompt:**

office workers, and community residents to take regular movement breaks
during school or work days.

**AI Output:**  
Created index.html as a responsive, standalone PhysTive webpage with movement ideas for school, work, and the neighborhood. It includes a 5-minute break timer with pause, resume, and end controls.

**Observation:**

for school, work, and the neighborhood. It also included a five-minute
break timer with pause, resume, and end controls. The result was a good
starting point, but the prompt did not clearly request a movement
checklist, weekly progress tracker, separate HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
files, accessibility requirements, or specific testing criteria.
